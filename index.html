About

Hello, I'm Jules.

I grew up trying to fix my own skin: severe breakouts, dryness, eczema. That led me to esthetics school, and years of learning and testing until skin became my expertise.

At Flora, skin is seen in context, from stress and sleep to seasons and everything in between. We work together to support your skin with high-performance, results-driven, plant-forward lines, because radiant skin starts with whole-body health.

I'm a Level 2 Acne Expert with Face Reality, one of only a few in the greater Spokane and Coeur d'Alene area, and I've helped hundreds of women heal their acne. In the treatment room I don't stick to one brand; I use the best products from a curated mix of professional lines. For home care, I carry Face Reality, Anfisa and GlyMed Plus.

FAQ

Good to know.

How do I book?
Every Book button on this site takes you to Jules' GlossGenius page, her booking platform. From there you can browse services, see open times and reserve your appointment. Gift cards are purchased through GlossGenius too.
Do you treat acne?
Yes. The Acne Facial is designed for active breakouts, combining purifying exfoliation (an enzyme or chemical peel), acne-focused extractions, high frequency and LED light therapy to soothe inflammation and support clear, balanced skin. Jules is a Level 2 Acne Expert with Face Reality. For long-term clarity, start with a Skin Consultation and she'll build a personalized home-care regimen for your skin.
What skincare brands do you carry?
Jules retails Face Reality, Anfisa and GlyMed Plus. During facials she uses a curated mix of professional brands, choosing the best products for your skin rather than sticking to one line. To order products, message Jules on Instagram or by email, then book a free Product Pickup.
What brow services do you offer?
Brow lamination, brow lamination and tint, brow wax, brow wax and tint, brow tweezing, and Brow Sculpt + Define, which combines lamination, shaping and a custom tint. Lip and chin waxing are also available.
Where is Flora located?
Flora Skin Wellness is located in a salon suite off the Argonne exit in Spokane Valley, at 8921 East Alki Avenue. It's a short drive from Spokane, Millwood and Liberty Lake, and easy to reach from Post Falls and Coeur d'Alene.
How should I show up to my appointment?
Come as you are, with a few small touches to help you get the most from your time. A bare face is ideal, so we can begin caring for your skin right away. Wear your hair in a way that's ready for a scalp massage, as it may get a little oil in it. And dress in something cozy, so you can stay peaceful and relaxed long after your facial ends.
What is the cancellation policy?
Appointments are reserved specifically for you. If you need to cancel or reschedule, please provide at least 72 hours notice. Cancellations or changes made within 72 hours of your appointment time, for any reason including illness, will result in a charge of 50% of the total cost of the service booked. Cancellations made the day-of appointment, or no-shows will result in a charge of 100% of the total cost of the service booked.
Are any services not pregnancy safe?
High frequency and the Botanical Scalp Treatment are not pregnancy safe. Let Jules know when booking and your facial will be adjusted.
How do virtual consultations work?
Before your consultation, Jules will reach out to you personally to gather everything she needs to know about your skin. Then the two of you will meet over video for a collaborative deep dive into your skin, its history, and your goals. Afterward, you'll receive personalized product recommendations, skin education, and lifestyle tips to help your skin change for good. Depending on your skin, a follow-up appointment may be recommended or needed.
How should I contact Jules?
Jules would love to hear from you. Send her a DM on Instagram at @floraskinwellness, or email floraskinwellness@gmail.com. She'll get back to you during business hours.
