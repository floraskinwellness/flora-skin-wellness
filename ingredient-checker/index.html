Is it pore-clogging?

Paste the ingredient list from any skincare, makeup or hair product and see if it contains ingredients known to clog pores. For each one we find, you'll see why it can cause congestion and its comedogenic rating.

About the ratings

The comedogenic scale.

Comedogenic means likely to clog pores. Ingredients are rated from 0 to 5 based on how often they caused clogged pores in lab testing:

  1. 0Won't clog pores
  2. 1Very low
  3. 2Moderately low
  4. 3Moderate
  5. 4Fairly high
  6. 5High

Not every ingredient has been lab-tested. Ingredients marked "Not lab-rated" are flagged because acne experts regularly see them cause breakouts in acne-prone skin. A rating depends on how much of an ingredient is in a formula, so treat it as a guide rather than a guarantee.
